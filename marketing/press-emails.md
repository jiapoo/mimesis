## 1. MacStories (John Voorhees)

**Subject:** Mimesis: compare two sports videos side by side on iPhone, iPad and Mac (indie, out 15 Dec)

```
Hi John,

This is my third app, after Elapsa and Imago: Mimesis, a sports video comparison app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It comes out on 15 December.

You film a swing, a serve or a stroke and open it next to a model: a coach, a pro, or a better day of your own. Sync points (racket drop, contact, finish) line the two clips up phase by phase, so they play together in slow motion even when one movement is faster. You can lay one over the other, see-through, mirror a left-hander, and draw lines and angles on any frame.

Mimesis Pro uses Vision on the device to cut the person out and lay them over the other clip, draw the skeleton with joint angles, and graph A against B through the movement. There's no account and no server.
```
