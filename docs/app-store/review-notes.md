# App Review notes

## Mimesis: Sports Video Compare (iPhone, iPad and Mac, one app record)

Mimesis compares two sports videos: side by side or one over the other, lined up by sync points, played together in slow motion, with drawings, angles and notes on the frames. Everything happens on the device. There is no account and no server.

HOW TO TEST
1. Open two videos: tap Files or Photos on the two empty cards (or drop two videos on the Mac window). Any two clips of a person moving work; videos filmed at 60 fps or more look best in slow motion.
2. Tap "A 🔗 B" in the bottom bar to link them, then Play. Speed (0.5× etc.) is on the right of the bar.
3. Tap Overlay at the top for one clip over the other, see-through.
4. Tap the pencil-in-a-circle button in the bar to draw: lines, arrows, circles and angles in degrees.
5. The camera-in-a-frame button puts a box over the clips; the camera takes a snapshot of what's inside.
A short screen recording of these steps is attached.

IN-APP PURCHASE
The app is free with no time limit. One non-consumable purchase unlocks Mimesis Pro on all the user's devices (universal purchase, Family Sharing off):
- com.ljworkshop.mimesis.pro.unlock ($24.99)
Mimesis Pro adds: the person cut out over the other clip (with alignment and tint), StroMotion, the skeleton with joint angles and the A-against-B graphs, the PDF report, takes over time, and exports without the small "Mimesis" mark that free exports carry.
To see the purchase screen, tap any of these: the figure button at the top of a clip (skeleton), "Measure the body in this segment" in the sidebar, "Cut out" in the overlay, StroMotion in a clip's ⋯ menu, or File > Report (PDF) on the Mac / Report in the ⋯ menu on iPhone and iPad.
"Restore Purchases" is on the purchase screen, in the Mimesis menu on the Mac, and in the ⋯ menu on iPhone and iPad. Opening and saving projects is never limited.
No subscription, no external payment links.

PERMISSIONS
- Camera (Live replay): shows the camera a few seconds late so a player can watch what they just did. Only used while Live replay is open. Nothing is recorded unless the user taps Save.
- Photos: the system photo picker only (no library access is requested).

SIMULATOR NOTE
The cut-out, the skeleton and lining up the bodies use Vision, which needs a real iPhone, iPad or Mac; in the iOS Simulator they show a message saying so.

ENCRYPTION
No encryption beyond what Apple's frameworks do. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is NO.
